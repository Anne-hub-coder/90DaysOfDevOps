# My 90-Day DevOps Learning Plan

## Current Level

I am currently a working professional who is transitioning into DevOps and Cloud Engineering. I have started this 90-day journey to build practical skills, gain hands-on experience, and prepare myself for a career as a DevOps Engineer.

## My Understanding of DevOps and Cloud Engineering

My understanding of DevOps is that it is a culture and practice that brings Development and Operations teams together to build, test, deploy, and maintain applications efficiently through collaboration and automation.

Cloud Engineering is the process of building, deploying, and managing applications and infrastructure using cloud platforms instead of relying on physical servers.

## Why I Am Learning DevOps & Cloud

I am learning DevOps and Cloud because I want to become a DevOps Engineer. I want to develop practical skills that are used in the real world, work with modern technologies, and prepare myself to crack DevOps interviews with confidence.


## My Goals for the Next 90 Days

1. Build a strong foundation in Linux, Git, Networking, and Cloud fundamentals.
2. Learn DevOps tools such as Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, and AWS through hands-on practice.
3. Build real-world projects and become confident enough to crack DevOps interviews and start my career as a DevOps Engineer.

## Core DevOps Skills I Want to Build

- Linux Administration and Troubleshooting
- Docker, Kubernetes, and Containerization
- CI/CD Pipeline Automation

## Weekly Study Plan

- Monday to Friday: 2 hours per day
- Saturday and Sunday: 4 hours per day

## My Commitment

For the next 90 days, I will stay consistent by studying every day, practicing every concept hands-on, completing each day's assignment, maintaining my notes, and continuously improving my skills. My focus is not only to complete this challenge but also to become a confident DevOps Engineer.
